City Council Approves Riverside Park Plan

By Jane Reporter, published Tuesday morning

After a debate that stretched past midnight, the council voted six to three to approve the long-awaited Riverside Park restoration, a sweeping project that will reshape the northern riverbank over the next four years.

The plan sets aside twelve acres for native meadows, restores the historic boathouse near the Maple Street entrance, and adds a continuous cycling path from the old mill all the way to the ferry landing.

Opponents argued the budget leaves too little for road repairs, but supporters pointed to the independent audit, which found that every dollar spent on green space returns nearly three dollars in nearby property value and tourism.

Construction begins in spring, starting with the drainage works along the eastern fence, and the mayor promised that the playground, the most beloved corner of the park, will stay open throughout the project.
